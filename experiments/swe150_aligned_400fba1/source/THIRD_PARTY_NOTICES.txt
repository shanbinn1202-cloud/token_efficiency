v0.24.0: https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/tree/v0.24.0
Its Apache-2.0 license and per-file copyright/SPDX notices are retained.
Local changes implement selective-prefix runtime integration; the review diff
is patches/v0.24.0.diff. The repository LICENSE does not replace upstream notices.

The model configuration and tokenizer assets under
source-overlay/remote_ra_linux/model-evidence/ are third-party assets from the
Qwen3.6-27B snapshot recorded in INSTALL_RELEASE.json:
revision 6a9e13bd6fc8f0983b9b99948120bc37f49c13e9.
Upstream model repository: https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.6-27B
model-config.json corresponds to config.json; tokenizer-config.json corresponds
to tokenizer_config.json; tokenizer.json retains its upstream name.
The installer checks these assets by SHA256. Model weights are not distributed.
Consult the upstream model repository's license and model-card conditions for
the snapshot before redistribution or use. This project's Apache-2.0 LICENSE
does not grant or replace rights in third-party model/tokenizer assets.

The dram_kv source and xuanxue_vllm_policy_adapter retain their upstream names
and notices. The captured dram-backed-kv LICENSE identifies Apache License 2.0
and "Copyright 2026 SnapKV experiments". Their algorithm integration is recorded in the source manifests;
the maintained Goal constructor uses its deterministic mode (rewriter=None).
Structured tool-call action normalization is a local integration change.

source-overlay/remote_ra_live_v2/terminus2_goal.py contains, verbatim, the
prompt templates terminus-json-plain.txt and timeout.txt of Harbor 0.21.0
(harbor/agents/terminus_2/templates/, SHA-256 pinned in that file) and short
literal strings of its terminus_2.py and tmux_session.py, used only to
recognise the requests of Harbor's Terminus 2 agent. Harbor 0.21.0 is
distributed under the Apache License 2.0 (its package metadata:
License-Expression: Apache-2.0).

Historical qualification references document a predecessor runtime. They do
not certify a newly built package or change the licenses of any bundled asset.

538026089d1a8a8eff05121d0db89b388f360eba, Copyright (c) 2026 Yuanzhe Hu): its SYSTEM_MESSAGE and its
long-context query template (utils/templates.py), in cases.py; and mini-swe-agent
(https://github.com/SWE-agent/mini-swe-agent, Copyright (c) 2025 Kilian A. Lieret and Carlos E.
Jimenez): its bash tool definition and the <returncode>/<output> observation format, in run_cases.py.
Their licenses are licenses/MemoryAgentBench-LICENSE and licenses/mini-swe-agent-LICENSE.
cases.py and score.py read MemoryAgentBench's utils/templates.py and utils/eval_other_utils.py from
the user's own checkout, SHA-256 pinned in examples/memoryagentbench/upstream/SHA256SUMS; those files
are not distributed here. No benchmark data is distributed here either: cases.py lists the MemAgent32
cases by row of the Hugging Face dataset cxkjntmngm/MemAgent_Bench (revision
a785de0f8d2511a43b9e524b5326e1105a4d218a), which declares no license, and builds them from the user's
own download.
