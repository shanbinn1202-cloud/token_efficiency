GPU-validated on one GH200: Qwen3.6-27B on the kv-session protocol with thinking off. Both jobs ran on 2026-10-17 at runtime commit `0d37346` and were verified offline on 2026-10-18. The verification archived with the evidence, the first, ran against the job harness of that time, whose pins and delivered code differ from the jobs', and reads `NOT_VERIFIED`. The verdicts below come from the re-verification with the jobs' own job harness, reconstructed for it (G2's `PMAB_PASS`: the status recomputed with the corrected check).

- **Job 3397844 (G1): `PMAB_PASS_VERIFIED`.** One synthetic case of 12 chunks (about 48K tokens of history) and its question, one session per server, the 262,144-token context, 24 GiB of GPU KV, eager execution: this runtime with `full` (the reference) and with `observation_query` at a 32,768-token budget, then stock vLLM 0.24.0, eager and with synchronous scheduling, replaying every prompt of the `full` server. Every turn of both runtime servers was token-exact against the client's construction; the stock replay generated the same tokens as `full` on all 13 turns, and `observation_query` gave the same replies as `full` on all 13. The protocol probes passed: a repeated and an unknown close (200), a turn-0 request for a closed id (400), a request for a busy session (400), a 262,144-token prompt refused with `CONTEXT_LIMIT` before any model work and the session usable afterwards, and `/health` (200). An optional stock server with vLLM's defaults, through the Vanilla transport of A.5, passed too.
- **Job 3397842 (G2): the runtime was correct; the job's verdict `PMAB_FAIL` came from a bug in one job-harness check, and with the corrected check the evidence recomputes offline to `PMAB_PASS`.** 16 sessions of `observation_query` at a 32,768-token budget with `--cuda-graphs full --decode-fastpath`, a 131,072-token context, `--dram-arena-gib 39` and `--release-idle-pages` ([docs/reference.md section 4.13](../reference.md#413-releasing-idle-sessions-pages-opt-in)), once with a 16-GiB and once with a 24-GiB pool: 32 synthetic cases of 5 to 7 chunks, 16 at a time, every case answered. 139 releases and 124 readmissions at 16 GiB, 13 and 13 at 24 GiB; nothing was preempted; all 32 cases were identical between the two pools, and every readmission restored exactly the Pages that a completed turn had committed. The failed check (route-b) counted releases that came after the engine had finished a turn, in the request's HTTP tail; with the corrected check the server passes. An optional third server without `--release-idle-pages` failed a turn for lack of blocks, as predicted; at the benchmark's runtime such a turn waits instead ([docs/running.md section 4.4](../running.md#44-several-sessions-on-one-server)).

**Run, scores pending:** the 32-case runs of this configuration (G4), all at runtime commit `f9322a6`, ran on 2026-10-18: job 3405844 (`observation_query`), job 3405845 (`goal_query` with the Goal rewriter) and job 3405855 (the stock Vanilla control). Route B released idle sessions' pages 385 times in job 3405844 and 460 times in job 3405845, and every readmission restored exactly the released pages; our runtime preempted nothing.
