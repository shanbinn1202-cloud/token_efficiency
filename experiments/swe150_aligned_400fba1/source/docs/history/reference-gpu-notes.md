## From section 4.10: admission optimizations

Moved from [section 4.10](../reference.md#410-admission-optimizations-opt-in) of docs/reference.md.

  - **Gain and cost.** A turn whose Goal takes longer than its phase 1 is admitted about the phase-1 time earlier. In job 3500604 (below) the overlap window, `goal_overlap_ms` from a Goal's start to its join, had a median of 170 ms on chat-text and 121 ms on mswea-xml; the saving is at most the smaller of `goal_construct_ms` and `goal_overlap_ms`, and the job did not measure it separately (median request times with and without the flag: 7.24 against 7.23 s on chat-text, 4.37 against 4.32 s on mswea-xml). A turn with a Goal forward costs one more probe-only pass.

  - **On the GPU (job 3500604, runtime `c07d42a`, one GH200; verified offline as `CMG1_PASS_VERIFIED`).** Two pairs of Qwen `goal_query` servers, without and with the flag, ran the same sessions in lockstep, each with `--admission-overlap 4`, `--release-idle-pages`, CUDA graphs `full` and the three decode fast paths: the strip history on chat-text (`--thinking-history strip`, 30 turns) and the keep mode on mswea-xml (`--enable-thinking` alone, 18 turns). The flag also ran with the vLLM rewriter in the failed SWE-bench run of job 3500565 (`--goal-rewriter-engine vllm`, below).

- **On the GPU (job 3349504, runtime `4eae441`, the admission work merged into this revision; one GH200; verified offline, token-exact).** `goal_query` with the Qwen3-0.6B rewriter at 5 and 16 sessions, `--cuda-graphs full`, 24 GiB KV. The offline selection replay does not apply; it covers `observation_query` and `random_fill` only. At 16 sessions, without the flags a request waited 48.3 s on average for admission (86 % of its request time) and the server completed 13.8 turns per minute.
